I'm Rachel Gross

ICI Postdoctoral Fellow | Department of Astronomy, University of Virginia

My research focuses on gas- and ice-phase chemistry in star- and planet-forming environments. I combine laboratory astrochemistry, JWST observations, chemical modeling, and radiative transfer to investigate how irradiation, temperature, and physical structure shape molecular abundances and volatile evolution.
